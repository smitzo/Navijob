Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one high-fidelity desktop UI screenshot for NaviJob, a real recruitment and job applications website. Flat front-facing interface, landscape around 1536 by 1024, no devices and no presentation-board framing. This is a dense but calm job search screen a professional would actually use every day.
Design direction: trustworthy professional recruitment platform, influenced by the information clarity of Ashby and Wellfound and the practical care of an established national jobs service. Crisp white, neutral pale-gray separators and a restrained deep royal blue #244A83. Distinctive humanist sans typography resembling Source Sans 3 or IBM Plex Sans, precise weight choices and readable body copy. Small corner radii, almost no shadows, sensible form fields and restrained blue actions.
Top header: lowercase "navijob" wordmark, "Jobs", "Companies", "My profile", "Applications", and a small initials account control. Below, the compact title "Your next role, in focus." Search controls "Role, skill or company" and "City or remote", button "Search jobs".
Main layout: left narrow filters for Work mode, Experience and Location; middle job list; right a clearly selected job-detail panel. Show three or four credible job entries using fictional companies Morrow Labs, Ledger House, Parcel Works. Selected entry: "Backend Engineer", "Morrow Labs", "Bengaluru · Hybrid", "₹18–28 LPA", "2–4 years", "Full-time". The right panel shows company monogram, "Backend Engineer", those same details, a compact dark-blue "Apply with your profile" button and a secondary Save button; then readable "About the role", "What you'll work on", "Skills" sections with short realistic text about Python, APIs, PostgreSQL and reliable backend services. Include an understated recruiter identity or posting source without an unverifiable badge. Small footer "Design concept · Sample roles".
Make the screen polished, functional, balanced and convincingly implemented, with excellent grid alignment, keyboard-friendly visible controls, a clear selection state, plain language and natural content density. No huge marketing hero, no fake application graphs, no generic AI sparkle motifs, no floating chips around headlines, no glass cards, no gradients, no glowing purple, no giant pill corners, no bento layout, no flashy startup art. UI craft should come from typography, hierarchy and proportion.
