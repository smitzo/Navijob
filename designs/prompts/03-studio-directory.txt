Use case: ui-mockup.
Generate one refined, high-fidelity desktop website prototype screenshot for NaviJob, a serious hiring and job application platform for software professionals. Flat desktop page, landscape around 1536 by 1024, no browser chrome, no laptop mockup or angled perspective. Use crisp legible functional UI text and an editorially composed grid.
Design direction: a distinctive professional talent directory informed by Swiss print design and the confident identity of creative recruitment networks such as Working Not Working. This is an authentic jobs product rather than a generic SaaS template. Mostly soft ivory #F5F3EB, ink #22231F, thin charcoal rules, with a single subdued yellow accent #E3D578 used on selected controls. Bold compact grotesk typography for navigation and job titles, readable humanist sans for descriptions, small mono-style index numbers and metadata. Flat panels, almost square corners, structured rhythm and generous gutters.
At top a strong lowercase "navijob." wordmark and understated navigation "Find work", "Explore companies", "Your profile", "Applications", with "Hire talent" as an outlined button. Beneath, a horizontal masthead with title "Good work. Better opportunities." at a moderate, usable size, a short sentence "Explore roles at teams building something that matters.", and no fabricated community statistics. Under this, an elongated search strip with Keyword, Location, and a yellow Search button.
The actual job directory should dominate: a slim vertical filter column, a wide orderly list of four roles with numbered rows, company monograms, role and company, annual INR compensation, work mode, experience, and simple Save actions. Fictional sample roles: "Backend Engineer / Morrow Labs / Bengaluru · Hybrid / ₹18–28 LPA"; "Full-stack Engineer / Parcel Works / Remote, India / ₹15–24 LPA"; "Platform Engineer / Northline / Pune · Hybrid / ₹22–35 LPA"; "Software Engineer / Ledger House / Mumbai · On-site / ₹12–20 LPA". Add a narrow right-hand profile rail titled "Your next step" with a simple candidate profile completion checklist, a quiet "Edit profile" link, and three small project title links; no match score, no progress-ring gimmicks. The page should feel like a confident professional publication with real controls and enough job detail to be useful. Footer note "Design concept · Sample roles".
Avoid generic black-and-purple AI styling, neon accents, gradients, glass, giant rounded cards, excessive badges, floating geometrical decorations, illustrations, AI agent references, dashboards full of irrelevant metrics, fabricated testimonials or partner logos. Achieve character through type, composition and consistent information design.
