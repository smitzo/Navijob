Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high fidelity original website design concept for NaviJob, an Indian recruitment and job application website. This is an image prototype, not an implementation. Render one flat front-facing desktop web page, 1536 by 1280 pixels if possible, crisp readable type and meticulous alignment. No laptop/device frame, perspective, gallery collage, external commentary or watermark. The visible page must include both the brand-led homepage and useful job browsing below the hero. It should feel designed by a strong independent web design studio, with a coherent recruitment identity.
Use only the NaviJob brand and fictional sample employers, not other companies' logos or testimonials. Navigation: Find jobs, For employers, How it works, Sign in, Create profile. Core promise: reuse one profile and choose a saved resume when applying to native NaviJob roles. AI agents, external ATS automation and paid priority applications are absent.
Keep sample jobs consistent: Backend Engineer / Fieldnote / Bengaluru, Hybrid / INR 12–18 LPA; Product Designer / Common Ground / Remote, India / INR 10–16 LPA; Frontend Engineer / Relay Studio / Pune, Hybrid / INR 8–14 LPA. Small visible label near listings: "Sample roles · Design preview". Clear View role actions. No fabricated user counts, success rates, employer endorsements or verification badges.
Avoid generic SaaS bento grids, purple/blue gradients, glow, glassmorphism, floating decorative UI chips, AI sparkles, 3D blobs, generic vector office people, vague motivational stock art, three-column job portal hero, cookie banners and tiny illegible metadata.

Art direction: bold, human, editorial recruitment brand. Visual research: the 2023 REDSOFA gallery entry on Lapa Ninja uses enormous grotesk brand lettering, strong coral and peach fields and documentary portraits. Translate that design confidence into an ORIGINAL NaviJob layout, without copying its composition, logo, text or portrait.
Composition: top 74px white navigation bar, custom confident lowercase navijob wordmark at left. Underneath a full-width pale coral field, with a dramatic oversized black line "Good people." and second line "Better possibilities." occupying the left two-thirds. Beside it, two tightly art-directed rectangular candid photographs of young Indian professionals collaborating in a bright studio; natural texture and real faces, no staged handshake. Strong asymmetry and generous margins, bold cropped typography but every word readable. At the bottom of this hero, a quiet black CTA "Find your next role ↗" and adjacent plain link "Build your profile", with one short explanatory sentence.
Below the hero a white recruitment section headed "A few good places to start." Set the three sample jobs in a sophisticated wide typographic list with horizontal rules, aligned salary and location columns, small square original employer marks, and unobtrusive arrow actions. A coral narrow label strip "One profile. Your projects. Ready when you are." anchors the bottom. Colour blocks are flat, predominantly coral #F46D59, warm white #FAF9F6 and ink #191919. Use confident wide grotesk typography with carefully balanced optical sizes, black readable body copy. This is a bold recruitment website, not an admin dashboard and not a traditional newspaper. Make the craft of the composition the selling point.
