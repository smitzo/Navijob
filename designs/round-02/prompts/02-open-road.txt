Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high fidelity original website design concept for NaviJob, an Indian recruitment and job application website. This is an image prototype, not an implementation. Render one flat front-facing desktop web page, 1536 by 1280 pixels if possible, crisp readable type and meticulous alignment. No laptop/device frame, perspective, gallery collage, external commentary or watermark. The visible page must include both the brand-led homepage and useful job browsing below the hero. It should feel designed by a strong independent web design studio, with a coherent recruitment identity.
Use only the NaviJob brand and fictional sample employers, not other companies' logos or testimonials. Navigation: Find jobs, For employers, How it works, Sign in, Create profile. Core promise: reuse one profile and choose a saved resume when applying to native NaviJob roles. AI agents, external ATS automation and paid priority applications are absent.
Keep sample jobs consistent: Backend Engineer / Fieldnote / Bengaluru, Hybrid / INR 12–18 LPA; Product Designer / Common Ground / Remote, India / INR 10–16 LPA; Frontend Engineer / Relay Studio / Pune, Hybrid / INR 8–14 LPA. Small visible label near listings: "Sample roles · Design preview". Clear View role actions. No fabricated user counts, success rates, employer endorsements or verification badges.
Avoid generic SaaS bento grids, purple/blue gradients, glow, glassmorphism, floating decorative UI chips, AI sparkles, 3D blobs, generic vector office people, vague motivational stock art, three-column job portal hero, cookie banners and tiny illegible metadata.

Art direction: lively but useful candidate-first job board, influenced by the inspected historical Parallel homepage in Landingfolio: strong rounded geometric typography, integrated search, visible outlined job cards and a confident contrast section. Reinterpret these principles in an ORIGINAL NaviJob design, not a copy of its headline, stickers or branding.
Composition: white narrow navigation, small custom navijob wordmark. Hero uses a flat soft butter yellow #FFF4A8 background, generous 70px page gutters and a two-column asymmetric composition: very large rounded near-black geometric sans headline on left reading "Less paperwork." then "More possibilities." A short grounded sentence below: "Build your profile once. Find the role that fits. Apply with the resume you choose." On the right a commissioned-feeling sparse black monoline wayfinding illustration: an elegant looping path connects three understated rectangular paper fragments labeled Profile, Projects, Next role; a little cobalt directional arrow at the endpoint. No generic cartoon people, no floating pill chips, no 3D.
A large real search control across the lower hero with visible inputs "Role or keyword" and "Location" plus a black "Explore jobs ↗" button. Under it, a slim near-black horizontal band with three clear short text items: "One reusable profile", "Your resume versions", "Applications in one place", separated by fine vertical lines, no icons or stats.
Below on white, heading "Find your next chapter" with compact functional category links, then the three sample jobs in substantial white rectangular cards outlined with a single precise black stroke, restrained 8px corners, plain employer initials and clear title, location, salary and View role button. Cards have breathing room, body copy large enough to read. Cobalt #2947D7 appears sparingly in small links and the path arrow. Flat yellow, white and ink dominate. Distinctive geometric type, no gradient, no childish visual noise. Show useful discovery content and polished original brand design.
