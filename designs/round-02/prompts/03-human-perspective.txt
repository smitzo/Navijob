Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high fidelity original website design concept for NaviJob, an Indian recruitment and job application website. This is an image prototype, not an implementation. Render one flat front-facing desktop web page, 1536 by 1280 pixels if possible, crisp readable type and meticulous alignment. No laptop/device frame, perspective, gallery collage, external commentary or watermark. The visible page must include both the brand-led homepage and useful job browsing below the hero. It should feel designed by a strong independent web design studio, with a coherent recruitment identity.
Use only the NaviJob brand and fictional sample employers, not other companies' logos or testimonials. Navigation: Find jobs, For employers, How it works, Sign in, Create profile. Core promise: reuse one profile and choose a saved resume when applying to native NaviJob roles. AI agents, external ATS automation and paid priority applications are absent.
Keep sample jobs consistent: Backend Engineer / Fieldnote / Bengaluru, Hybrid / INR 12–18 LPA; Product Designer / Common Ground / Remote, India / INR 10–16 LPA; Frontend Engineer / Relay Studio / Pune, Hybrid / INR 8–14 LPA. Small visible label near listings: "Sample roles · Design preview". Clear View role actions. No fabricated user counts, success rates, employer endorsements or verification badges.
Avoid generic SaaS bento grids, purple/blue gradients, glow, glassmorphism, floating decorative UI chips, AI sparkles, 3D blobs, generic vector office people, vague motivational stock art, three-column job portal hero, cookie banners and tiny illegible metadata.

Art direction: refined, people-first recruitment brand, inspired by the inspected Frontify careers page on Land-book: expressive editorial serif typography, abundant space, documentary workplace photography, thin dividers and calm editorial rhythm. Create an ORIGINAL NaviJob identity and composition rather than reproducing its headline or image arrangement.
Composition: a quiet pale cool-grey page #F1F2EF, generous 64px margins and a precise nav bar. NaviJob wordmark is a compact dark ink serif. Main hero is intentionally restrained and spacious: large high-contrast elegant serif headline in the left 60% reading "There’s more to you" on one line and "than a résumé." on the next. On the right a 3-line humanist sans paragraph about projects, experience and one reusable profile, then a solid dark navy button "Create your profile ↗" and a small plain "Browse jobs" link. This is editorial composition, not a centered SaaS hero.
Immediately below, a wide shallow documentary photo strip, approximately 250px high, showing a small diverse Indian product team in a light-filled authentic office, quiet natural expressions and candid work at a table, film-like clarity without cliché gestures. Integrated at the left edge a modest caption "The people behind the work." No floating cards or fake testimonial.
Below photograph, a contrasting white open roles section split into a narrow left editorial introduction headed "Find where you belong." and a broad right 75% typographic job list of the three sample roles, clear fine horizontal rules, navy role titles, normal-size compensation and location, deliberate View role actions. Role list is the functional proof; maintain strong reading hierarchy and generous row height. Final small pale blue #DDE6E9 band shows "Your profile, ready for the next opportunity." with a simple profile document miniature integrated in the grid, not hovering. Palette cool paper, white, deep ink #182731 and pale slate-blue; no beige/oxide or bright portal blue theme. Elegant serif used for headlines only; highly legible sans for functional details. No huge unsupported brand numbers or promises.
