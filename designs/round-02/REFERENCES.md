## What changes from round 01

The earlier studies began with job-portal columns and changed their visual treatment. This round begins with a distinct recruitment identity and a designed homepage, with useful role information visibly integrated below the hero. It explores three different compositions, type approaches and uses of imagery.

Every concept must still expose the practical promise: reusable profile, saved resume choice and native applications. AI agents, automatic external applications and paid ranking are not introduced through the visuals.

## Review boundaries

The images are art-direction proposals. They do not establish exact font licensing, contrast compliance, responsive behavior, interactive states or implemented application flows. Those checks and the remaining product screens follow the user's explicit selection in M01. Do not promote a concept to approved merely because it was generated or passed image-file validation.

Visual inspection: all three concepts contain readable sample roles and clear actions. A uses a coral split hero and condensed display lettering; B uses integrated search and outlined cards; C uses serif display type, a documentary photo strip and a ruled list. All three are saved as 1374 × 1145 PNGs with exact prompts and a file manifest; the tool's output dimensions differ from the requested dimensions.

Copy refinement after selection: replace the generated term "native NaviJob roles" with everyday wording such as "jobs accepting applications on NaviJob". C's salary display repeats the currency symbol and INR; use one format consistently. Profile thumbnails, sample candidate identity, dates, role descriptions and font details are illustrative and must be reviewed before any implementation. These copy issues do not select or reject the visual direction on the user's behalf.
