## Why

A curated recruitment platform needs operational checks, transparent evidence labels and a verified first-release journey.

## What Changes

- Build operator review for companies, roles and optional public profiles.
- Add reports, scoped verification labels, export/deletion request handling and audit history.
- Complete visual, accessibility and end-to-end readiness review with demo/live separation.

Dependencies: M03, M05, M06. Non-goals: Claiming certifications or universal candidate verification, enterprise scale, live launch or external outreach in the planning turn.
