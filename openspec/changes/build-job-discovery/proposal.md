## Why

The platform must make real roles easy to compare through clear metadata and dependable search behavior.

## What Changes

- Build public job search, filtering, sorting, pagination and role details.
- Build company context pages and authenticated saved jobs.
- Distinguish approved native roles, external source listings, closed roles and unknown compensation.

Dependencies: M02. Non-goals: AI matching percentages, paid ranking, unpermitted scraping or implied employer partnerships.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `job-discovery`: Job discovery and company pages.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing application behavior must be audited rather than presumed production-ready.

## Impact

backend jobs/companies APIs, frontend job discovery/detail/company screens. This is a proposed milestone; implementation tasks remain open.
